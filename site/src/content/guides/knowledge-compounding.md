---
description: How ab-knowledge-compounding records a solved problem in docs/solutions/, and how later design and research work finds it.
---

[`ab-knowledge-compounding`](/skills/ab-knowledge-compounding/) writes each solved problem up with its root cause, the solution, the approaches that failed and the terms to search for. [`ab-build-pipeline`](/skills/ab-build-pipeline/) and [`ab-ship-pipeline`](/skills/ab-ship-pipeline/) run it after review when the work solved a non-trivial problem, and you can ask for it after any fix whose lesson the code, the tests and the commit message do not carry.
