---
description: What Agent Blueprint installs in your tool, which files ab-project-start adds to your project, and what each docs/ folder is for.
---

The plugin lives in your tool and puts no files in your project. Your project gets its files from the `ab-project-start` skill: an `AGENTS.md` that every tool reads, and a `docs/` tree where plans, decisions and solved problems stay from one session to the next.
